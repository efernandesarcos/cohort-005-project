# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary fixes the words we use for buying a course and getting money back.

## Language

### Buying a course

**Student**:
A person who takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: learner, user, buyer

**Purchase**:
A Student paying for a course.
_Avoid_: order

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to a course. A Student can have an Enrollment without having made the Purchase themselves.
_Avoid_: access, subscription

**Admin**:
A person who runs Cadence itself, as opposed to an Instructor, who owns courses.
_Avoid_: staff

**Course Progress**:
The share of a course's lessons a Student has completed. It belongs to the Student, not the Enrollment, so it stays when an Enrollment ends and is there again if they re-enroll.
_Avoid_: completion, progress percentage

**Watch Time**:
How much of a course's video a Student has watched. Like Course Progress, it belongs to the Student and outlives an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: hours watched, seconds watched

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Student's request, with a reason, to get back what they paid for a Purchase, which an Admin reviews by hand and either approves or declines. A Purchase can have only one Refund Request, and a Student cannot make one for a course they have already had a Refund Request Approved on.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the Student once a Refund Request is Approved. A Declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Amount**:
What a Refund pays back: always the amount the Student actually paid for the Purchase, never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: refundable amount

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, last day included, in which the Student can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: cancel window, cancellation window

**Requested**:
A Refund Request that is waiting for a decision. Every Refund Request starts here, and the Student keeps their Enrollment while it waits.
_Avoid_: Pending, Open

**Approved**:
A Refund Request that an Admin accepted. It is final: approval ends the Student's Enrollment in that course at once, and a Refund is owed.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request that an Admin turned down, with a reason the Student is told. It is final: no Refund is paid and the Student keeps their Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied
