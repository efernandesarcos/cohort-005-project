# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary fixes the words we use for buying a course and getting money back.

## Language

### Buying a course

**Student**:
A person who takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: learner, user, buyer

**Purchase**:
A Student paying for a course.
_Avoid_: order

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to a course. A Student can have an Enrollment without having made the Purchase themselves.
_Avoid_: access, subscription

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Student's request, with a reason, to get back what they paid for a Purchase, which staff review by hand and either approve or decline.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the Student once a Refund Request is approved; it ends the Student's Enrollment in that course. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Amount**:
What a Refund pays back: always the amount the Student actually paid for the Purchase, never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: refundable amount

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, last day included, in which the Student can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: cancel window, cancellation window

**Requested**:
A Refund Request that is waiting for a decision. Every Refund Request starts here.
_Avoid_: Pending, Open

**Approved**:
A Refund Request that was accepted, so a Refund is paid.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request that was turned down, so no Refund is paid.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied
